This Privacy Policy explains how Coruf handles information when you use the app. Coruf is a marketing studio for campaign briefs, Credits, Sample Lab rehearsals, and campaign kit text.

1. Who we are

Coruf is published for iPhone. Questions about this policy or a privacy request go to coruf.desk.ruxl@icloud.com.

2. Information the app keeps

Coruf keeps the following on your iPhone:

  • A Quick Login username and password created on the device the first time you sign in.
  • Whether you accepted the Terms of Service, whether a session is active, and whether you have logged out.
  • Your Credits balance, ledger lines, and App Store transaction identifiers used to avoid granting the same purchase twice.
  • Campaign drafts and finished kits (product name, selling points, languages, modules, and the composed kit text).
  • Sample Lab partner threads, your replies, hidden notes, and block or report outcomes stored for your account on the device.
  • Voice notes, rehearsal recordings, and kit text files you save in the app’s documents folder.
  • Photos you attach to a rehearsal, held for that session until you delete them.

Coruf does not ask you to enter a payment card number. Purchases are completed by Apple.

3. Permissions

The system asks for a permission when you use the related feature.

  • Camera. Live interpretation rehearsal, and attaching a still to a campaign kit.
  • Microphone. Dictation on a campaign brief, voice notes, and live interpretation rehearsal.
  • Speech recognition. Turning dictation and spoken rehearsal lines into text. Apple’s speech recognition service may process the audio to return a transcript.
  • Photo Library. Choosing stills and reels to attach to a rehearsal or kit.
  • Photo Library add. Saving a campaign still when you tap Save Media.
  • Notifications. The first time Studio appears, Coruf asks for alert, badge, and sound permission through the system dialog. There is no separate notification settings screen in the app.

4. Facial data

This chapter explains collection, use, disclosure, sharing, and retention of facial data for Coruf.

4.1 What is collected

Coruf does not collect biometric faceprints, face geometry templates, or facial recognition identifiers.

The camera preview in a live interpretation rehearsal can show whatever the lens sees, which may include your face if you point the camera at yourself. That preview is used to show the rehearsal. Coruf does not derive a face template from it.

If you attach a photo or save a still, the image file may depict a face. Those files are ordinary photos you chose or saved. They are not scanned to build a biometric profile.

4.2 How facial imagery is used

Camera imagery is used to display the live rehearsal and, when you record a demo clip, to store that clip as a movie you can play or delete. Photos you attach are shown in the rehearsal so you can review them. A still you save with Save Media is written to your Photo Library because you asked to save it.

Coruf does not use facial imagery to identify you, to advertise to you, or to train a face model.

4.3 Disclosure and sharing

Coruf does not sell facial imagery and does not send your rehearsal photos or camera frames to a generative model service.

If speech recognition is running during a rehearsal, microphone audio — not a face template — may be processed by Apple’s speech recognition service as described in section 3. Saving a still uses Apple’s Photos library on your device.

4.4 Where it is stored and how long it is kept

A camera preview is shown while the rehearsal is open. A demo clip you record is stored as a movie file in the app’s documents folder until you delete the clip or erase the account. Photos you attach for a rehearsal remain available in that session until you delete them. A still saved with Save Media remains in your Photo Library under your control.

Erase All App Data removes the app’s UserDefaults domain for com.coruf.ruxl and deletes files in the app’s documents folder, including recordings and kit text files you saved there. Photos already saved to the Photo Library are not removed from the library by that action; you delete those in Photos.

4.5 Scope of this chapter

Section 4, including §§4.1–4.5, is Coruf’s explanation of the collection, use, disclosure, sharing, and retention of facial data.

5. Campaign text and speech

When you finish a campaign kit, Coruf composes kit text on the iPhone from the product name, selling points, languages, and modules you entered. That composition does not send your brief, photos, or kit text to a third-party generative model API.

Microphone audio used for dictation or for checking a spoken rehearsal line may be sent to Apple’s speech recognition service so the app can receive a transcript. Coruf uses the transcript to fill a brief or to compare a spoken line with the rehearsal line shown on screen.

6. Sample Lab and reports

Sample Lab shows partner profiles and message threads. You can reply, attach a photo, record a voice note, report a note, block a partner, or remove a note from your lab.

Before a reply is posted, Coruf checks the text against a keyword blocklist. A reply that contains a blocked phrase is not posted.

Report opens a mail draft to coruf.desk.ruxl@icloud.com that includes the partner name and the reported note, and removes that partner from your lab on the device. Block asks you to confirm, then removes that partner’s notes from your lab. Your own notes can be deleted from the more menu. If you send the mail, your mail app delivers it. The message content is whatever your mail draft contains.

8. Notifications

The first time you open Studio, Coruf requests notification authorization. The app does not include a separate button that asks again, and it does not implement a notification response handler.

9. Children

Coruf is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, contact coruf.desk.ruxl@icloud.com.

10. Sale of personal information

Coruf does not sell personal information.

11. Delete account

Settings includes Erase All App Data. After you confirm, Coruf deletes the Quick Login credentials, Credits, ledger, campaign kits, lab history, agreement flag, and files in the app documents folder. You return to the welcome screen. This does not delete photos you already saved to the Photo Library and does not refund Apple purchases.
